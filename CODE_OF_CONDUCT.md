# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.
Contributors must communicate respectfully, accept constructive feedback,
focus on what is best for users, and protect private or sensitive information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces or when representing the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security/contact channels. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions,
comments, issues, or other material and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Reports will be handled with discretion and conflicts of
interest will be disclosed or reassigned.
